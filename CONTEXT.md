# DevRecall

DevRecall keeps a local library of technical pages so people can find useful material again.

## Language

**Saved page**:
A local library entry created from a captured technical page.
_Avoid_: Bookmark, document

**Manual save**:
A saved page capture explicitly requested by a person, whether from the side panel or a keyboard shortcut.
_Avoid_: Menu save, shortcut save

**Content chunk**:
An ordered segment of a saved page's extracted body text.
_Avoid_: Snippet, passage

**Page metadata**:
Descriptive information attached to a saved page rather than extracted from its body. This includes its title, summary, topics, technologies, site, and content type.
_Avoid_: Content
